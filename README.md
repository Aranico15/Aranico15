## 👑 About

I'm the founder and CEO of **Crown Nifix**, an independent technology organization that brings together talent in digital development, experience and interface design, data processing, structural systems, artificial intelligence and corporate branding.

I study **Data Engineering & Artificial Intelligence** at Universidad Santo Tomás (Tunja). I like turning ideas into real, scalable systems with product potential.
